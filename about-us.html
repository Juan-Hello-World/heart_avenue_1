Education & Creativity

We focus on reading routes, literacy programs, and story readings, encouraging children to create their own stories. We also promote art dissemination, organize medical activities, and run essential donation campaigns.

Global Reach

Our programs have a far-reaching impact, operating in a hybrid model that combines international virtual initiatives with in-person programs across more than 10 countries worldwide.

Romanticism & Art

We have a deep commitment to artistic expression and creative work, especially with children. Our workshops and projects are crafted to create a safe space for authentic self-expression.
