Heart Avenue

Heart Avenue is an international non-profit association focused on the protection of children and women and their development in literacy, health & arts. With operational presence in global regions, we work to improve community well-being, education, and promote culture diversity. Founded in April 2025, we are a charity with a strong commitment to cultural and artistic development, seeking to create safe spaces and provide social support for vulnerable populations.

Build Your House in Heart Avenue

When you found a House, it functions as an official operational branch of Heart Avenue. You become part of our Board of Directors, maintaining creative identity and operational freedom, while receiving full institutional support, creative protection, access to our international network, contests, magazine features, and platform benefits. Lead projects in your community and join the mission!
