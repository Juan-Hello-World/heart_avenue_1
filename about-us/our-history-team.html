Our funding history

Heart Avenue Association, founded in April 2025 by Edelweiss Dryadis, began with a vision for the cultural, artistic, medical, and educational development of children and women in vulnerable situations. Our journey started even earlier with a test project in December 2024 under the name "Ninsun Foundation." This initial step paved the way for our current collaborative framework, empowering local chapters to lead regional initiatives and now with a global network and chapters in Bahrain, USA, South Africa, Colombia and Peru.

Core Purpose

Our Mission

To provide opportunities for comprehensive development and protection to children and women in vulnerable situations through education, health, and cultural-artistic expression.

Future Goal

Our Vision

Establish a strong infrastructure of shelters and educational centers with our own curriculum focused on psychosocial and medical well-being, specializing in the protection and prevention of violence against women and children.
